Some of this information, such as your IP address, may constitute personal information under applicable law, and we treat it accordingly.

### c. Chatbot Data

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of any inaccurate information.
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal obligations.

- **Right to Object**: Object to certain processing of your personal information, including its use for training our chatbot.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at **steven@xiluo.net**. We may need to verify your identity or ask for information to locate your data (for example, the approximate date and time of a chatbot conversation). We will respond within a reasonable time.

**Complaints**: If you have a complaint about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner ([www.oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au)) or the data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

## 9. Chatbot Data Processing
- To generate responses, the content of your chatbot conversations is sent to third-party AI service providers. The specific provider used may change from time to time. These providers process the content in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies, which may include retaining data for a limited period.
- We store the full content of chatbot conversations, and retain it indefinitely unless you request deletion.
- Conversation data may be reviewed and analysed by us, and used to train and improve our chatbot and AI systems.
- Stored conversations are not fully anonymised. We may take reasonable steps to remove or reduce personally identifiable information where practicable, but we cannot guarantee that all such information will be removed.
- **Please do not share sensitive personal information** (such as health information, financial account details, government identifiers, passwords, or information about other people) with the chatbot.
- You may request deletion of your chatbot conversations by contacting us. Please provide enough detail (such as the approximate date, time, and content of the conversation) for us to locate them. Deletion from our records does not affect data already processed or retained by third-party AI service providers under their own policies, or data already incorporated into trained models.
